博士研究生 · 中国科学院自动化研究所 Ph.D. Student · Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences

我目前是中国科学院自动化研究所模式识别与智能系统专业的博士研究生，联合培养单位为北京中关村学院 × 中关村人工智能研究院。研究兴趣包括计算机视觉、多模态大模型与 Diffusion 模型，同时关注具身智能中的视觉表征学习。

本科毕业于深圳大学自动化专业，获优秀毕业生与荣誉学士学位。曾在香港科技大学（广州）、深圳北理莫斯科大学人工智能研究院等机构从事科研实习，相关工作发表于 CVPR 2026 与 ICCV 2025。

I am a Ph.D. student majoring in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), jointly trained with Zhongguancun Academy × Zhongguancun Institute of Artificial Intelligence. My research interests include computer vision, multimodal large models and diffusion models, as well as visual representation learning for embodied AI.

I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Shenzhen University, graduating as an Outstanding Graduate with an Honors Degree. I have done research internships at HKUST (Guangzhou) and the AI Institute of Shenzhen MSU-BIT University, with work published at CVPR 2026 and ICCV 2025.

02发表论文Publications

CVPR 2026

Bootstrap Dynamic-Aware 3D Visual Representation for Scalable Robot Learning

Qiwei Liang, Boyang Cai, Minghao Lai, Sitong Zhuang, Tao Lin, Yan Qin, Yixuan Ye, Jiaming Liang, Renjing Xu

IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2026

提出 AFRO 自监督框架，通过特征差分与逆一致性监督学习具有动力学感知能力的 3D 表征，无需动作标签或显式重建，提升机器人在仿真与真实任务中的操作成功率与扩展性。

AFRO, a self-supervised framework that learns dynamics-aware 3D representations via feature differencing and inverse-consistency supervision, without action labels or explicit reconstruction, improving manipulation success and scalability in simulation and the real world.

arXivProject
ICCV 2025

OVG-HQ: Online Video Grounding with Hybrid-modal Queries

Runhao Zeng, Jiaqi Mao, Minghao Lai, Minh Hieu Phan, Yanjie Dong, Wei Wang, Qi Chen, Xiping Hu

IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV), 2025

提出在线混合模态查询视频定位新任务，支持文本、图像、视频片段及其组合的实时定位；设计统一框架 OVG-HQ-Unify（参数记忆块 + 跨模态蒸馏），并构建 QVHighlights-Unify 数据集与在线评估指标。

A new task of online video grounding with hybrid-modal queries (text, image, video clip and their combinations). We propose OVG-HQ-Unify with a Parametric Memory Block and cross-modal distillation, plus the QVHighlights-Unify dataset and online evaluation metrics.
